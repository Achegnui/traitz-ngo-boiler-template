# Publishing a news post (one page)

**1. Sign in.** Go to your website address followed by `/admin`. Enter your email and password and press *Sign in*. Forgot it? Type your email, then press *Forgot password?*.

**2. Start a post.** Press *Write a news post* on the Dashboard (or News, then *New news post*).

**3. Fill it in.**
- Every text box has an **English** and a **Français** side. English is required. If French is empty, French visitors see the English.
- *Title*, *Category*, *Short summary* (one or two sentences, shown on cards and in Google) and *Article text* (leave a blank line between paragraphs).
- *Cover photo*: press *Upload image*, describe it in a few words, and tick the permission box only if you really may publish the photo.
- The page address fills in from the title.

**4. Publish.** Press *Publish*. If something is missing, a red box lists it; click an item to jump to it. The post is live at once. Not ready? Press *Save draft* (drafts never appear on the site). Contributors can only save drafts; an editor publishes them.

**5. Change or remove it.** News, then *Edit*, then *Save changes*. *Unpublish* hides it; *Delete* removes it permanently.

**Good to know.** Pages are built from sections (Pages in the menu): open one to edit, Up/Down to reorder, *Add a section* at the bottom. Photos: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF up to 10 MB. Reports: PDF up to 20 MB. Administrators add people and change roles under Users.
