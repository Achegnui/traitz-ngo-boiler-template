# Photo slots

Every place where the site shows a photograph, with the shape to shoot and the size to upload. Today each slot shows an illustrated stand-in (drawn in code by `lib/art.tsx`) or a CSS gradient, so there are no image files to swap: you upload a real photo and the stand-in disappears.

**How to replace one.** Admin > open the item (or Pages > the section) > the image field > Upload image. Then write the alt text in English and French (describe what is in the photo; leave it empty only for pure decoration) and tick the permission box. The upload goes to Cloudinary and `next/image` serves it as AVIF or WebP at the right size for each screen.

**Sizes.** The pixel sizes below are what to upload (about 2x the largest size shown, so screens stay sharp). Shoot or crop to the aspect ratio shown, JPEG or WebP, under 10 MB. Keep the main subject near the centre where the table says so: some places crop the image.
